## Welcome and orientation draft

Welcome to the 28-Day Fat Loss Accelerator. You are going to work through the same seven-day
sequence four times: five workouts, one active-recovery day, and one full rest day.

That repetition is on purpose. The goal is not to surprise you with 28 different workouts. The goal
is to help you get better at the same work: cleaner reps, better control, more confidence, and more
capacity.

Go at your pace. Take breaks. Use the modifications that let you train safely and come back
tomorrow. When you have completed a day by your own standard, tap Complete Day in the app. The next
day opens on the next calendar day. If life makes you miss a day, you do not lose your place and the
program does not skip ahead.

All you need is a jump rope, enough room to move, and your bodyweight. A mat or soft surface is
optional for the floor work.

Starting weight and waist measurements are optional. Use them if they help you, skip them if they
do not. They will never block the program.

Do not try to prove everything on Day 1. Set your baseline, stay consistent, and let the four weeks
do their job. When you are ready, begin Day 1.

## Weekly coaching drafts

### Week 1: Set Your Baseline

This week is about learning the workouts and finding your honest starting point. Do not turn the
first round into a test you have to pass. Notice where you need a break, which movements need a
modification, and what pace you can control. Complete each day by your standard and give yourself a
baseline you can improve next week.

### Week 2: Clean It Up

You know the workouts now, so this week is about quality. Clean up your rope rhythm, your bodyweight
form, and your transitions. A controlled rep is worth more than a rushed rep. If you improve your
form and waste less motion, you are progressing even if the number on the timer does not change.

### Week 3: Raise Your Output

This week, choose one way to do a little more. That might mean a slightly quicker rope pace, one or
two more good reps, a shorter reset, or fewer unnecessary pauses. Pick one lever, not all of them.
The goal is a controlled increase you can repeat, not one heroic workout that wrecks the rest of
your week.

### Week 4: Finish Strong

You are in the final week. Finish strong by finishing consistently. Use what you learned, trust the
pace you built, and do not throw away three good weeks by forcing sloppy reps. Optional final
measurements will be there at the end if you want them. More important, notice what now feels
cleaner, stronger, or more manageable than it did in Week 1.

## New Workout F outline

### Recording rules

- Call it **Workout F: Active Recovery** and name the 28-Day Fat Loss Accelerator in the opening.
- Keep the effort easy enough for the customer to speak in full sentences.
- Offer ghost rope, marching, or walking as equal options whenever the rope is used.
- Include the warm-up in the recording.
- Avoid calorie-burn numbers, medical claims, weight-loss promises, other-program promotion, and
  temporary contact information.
- End by reminding the customer that tomorrow is a full rest day and that the app advances only
  when they acknowledge today's assignment.

### Timed structure

1. **Welcome and effort check - 30 seconds**
   - Explain that today is easy movement, not another hard workout.
2. **Warm-up - 2 minutes**
   - March in place.
   - Shoulder rolls forward and backward.
   - Gentle torso turns.
   - Calf and ankle movement.
3. **Block 1 - 3 rounds**
   - 20 seconds easy rope, ghost rope, or walking.
   - 20 seconds march in place.
   - 20 seconds easy rest while continuing to move.
4. **Mobility reset - 60 seconds**
   - Slow shoulder rolls and relaxed breathing.
5. **Block 2 - 3 rounds**
   - 20 seconds easy rope, ghost rope, or walking.
   - 20 seconds standing torso turns.
   - 20 seconds easy rest while continuing to move.
6. **Mobility reset - 60 seconds**
   - Gentle calf stretch, 30 seconds per side.
7. **Block 3 - 3 rounds**
   - 20 seconds easy rope, ghost rope, or walking.
   - 20 seconds alternating step-back reach without a deep lunge.
   - 20 seconds easy rest while continuing to move.
8. **Cool-down and close - 90 seconds**
   - Walk or march until breathing is comfortable.
   - Remind the customer that tomorrow is the full rest day.

## Customer-facing written orientation

The Accelerator repeats one seven-day sequence for four weeks: Workouts A-E, optional Active
Recovery F, and a full rest day. Repeating the workouts is how you improve pace, control, form,
capacity, and consistency.

Complete each day by your own standard. Take breaks and use modifications whenever you need them.
Watching a video does not automatically complete a day. Tap Complete Day when you are done, and the
next assignment opens on the next calendar day. Missing a day never skips your place or ends your
access.

You need a jump rope and room to move. A mat or soft surface is optional. Starting weight and waist
measurements are also optional and never block Day 1.

## Practical daily instructions

### Workout A: Classic Intervals

Alternate timed rope work with push-ups and lower-body movements across three circuits. The video
includes the warm-up and timer. Use regular bounce whenever a rope variation is not comfortable,
use knee push-ups when needed, and take additional breaks without worrying about keeping up with
Todd. Follow Todd's spoken correction when a timer label differs from the movement he demonstrates.

Equipment: jump rope; optional mat or soft surface.

### Workout B: EMOM

Warm up before pressing play. Each minute begins with 50 rope skips followed by the assigned core
or conditioning movement. Whatever remains in the minute is recovery. Reduce the skip or rep count
when needed so you can work with control. The final four-minute rope and squat-pulse finisher is
optional.

Equipment: jump rope; optional mat or soft surface.

### Workout C: Lower Body Ladder

The video includes the warm-up and timer. Work through rope-plus-squat, rope-plus-reverse-lunge,
and rope-plus-glute-bridge ladders, then finish with a short lower-body and core circuit. Use the
movement Todd demonstrates when a spoken count or timer cue differs. Move through each rep with
control and let the scheduled break be your recovery.

Equipment: jump rope; optional mat or soft surface.

### Workout D: Intervals

The video includes the warm-up and timer. Three two-round circuits alternate 30-second rope or
cardio intervals with push-ups, squats, lunges, glute bridges, and calf raises. Choose regular
bounce or another comfortable rope step whenever a variation is too complex. Good form matters
more than reaching every suggested rep.

Equipment: jump rope; optional mat or soft surface.

### Workout E: Pyramid Challenge

The video includes the warm-up and timer. Complete three up-and-down pyramids pairing 50 rope skips
with squats, push-ups, and mountain climbers. Finish with two rounds of rope, glute bridges, and
plank shoulder taps. Reduce the skips or bodyweight reps when necessary, and treat the final single-
leg glute-bridge option as optional.

Equipment: jump rope; optional mat or soft surface.

### Workout F: Active Recovery

The video is optional, but acknowledging the day is required. If you press play, keep the effort
easy enough to speak in full sentences. Use easy rope, ghost rope, marching, or walking as equal
options. The purpose is light movement and mobility, not another hard training day.

Equipment: jump rope optional; no gym equipment.

### Rest day

No workout is assigned. Take the full recovery day. Acknowledge the day in the app when you are
ready to continue; no video or exercise is required.
